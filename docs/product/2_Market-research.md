# Market research — competitors comparison (AI pantry + expiry + recipes + price)

## Competitive landscape (what exists today)

Most solutions cluster into 3 groups:

1. **Consumer pantry + expiry managers (barcode/photo scan, expiry alerts, family sharing)**
    
    Strong on inventory + notifications; weaker on receipts, price comparison, and advanced waste analytics.
    
2. **Recipe-first “AI meal planning from ingredients” apps**
    
    Strong on recipe generation and grocery lists; weaker on expiry accuracy, receipts, and waste measurement.
    
3. **Business/restaurant inventory & waste platforms**
    
    Strong analytics + multi-user workflows; not designed for households.

## Key takeaways (actionable gaps you can own)

1. **Spain-specific shelf-life intelligence is a real differentiator**
    
    Most apps do “generic expiry reminders.” Your idea of aligning inferred expiry to **Spanish supermarket norms** and learning “default expiry windows” per product is a concrete wedge.
    
2. **Confidence/uncertainty UX is underused**
    
    Your requirement (“AI must say it’s an estimate and ask the user”) is an excellent trust-builder. Many apps hide uncertainty; make it explicit with a lightweight confirmation flow.
    
3. **Household sharing needs a real event log**
    
    Competitors often support “sharing,” but not always “someone consumed it” traceability + notifications. A clear **consumption audit trail** is a strong value prop for couples/households.
    
4. **Waste analytics is usually shallow**
    
    “Total saved” claims are common; fewer products provide **drilldowns by time + category + item**, “at risk soon,” and **top-wasted items**. Your analytics vision is more advanced than most consumer apps.
    
5. **Price comparison is not standard in pantry apps**
    
    Some mention price tracking, but true **cross-supermarket/brand comparisons + alternatives** is less common—this could be a second major pillar (but it’s data-partnership heavy).
