# [C1] T-C1-91 · `DeflectionRecord` — domain, persistence, minimal-data rule and the recording use case

## Context

`US-C1-30` requires that when a requester opens a suggested article and then abandons the submission, a deflection record is created naming the article, the category and the timestamp — and that because **no Incident is created, the deflection is recorded independently of the Incident aggregate**. It requires the record to carry **no personal data beyond what support needs** (`NFR-SEC-07`), and states that the reporting surface belongs to `C17` / `C9`: **this story delivers only the recording at intake**.

**Finding F26 applies.** `FR-INC-16` requires deflection recording while §14.5 places deflection *measurement* (`FR-KNW-06`) in Phase 3 and the intake-facing articles in Phase 1. The two halves are being phased apart; `US-C1-29` and `US-C1-30` are separate stories precisely so they can be phased independently. That is a Product Owner sequencing decision, not a blocker on this ticket.

## Scope

- `DeflectionRecord` aggregate in `libs/incident/domain`: article reference, category identifier, `ClockPort` timestamp, and the fact that suggestions were shown — with no free-text content and no identifying data beyond what support needs.
- `RecordDeflectionUseCase` and `RecordSuggestionsShownUseCase` in `libs/incident/application`; the latter marks the Incident when a requester saw suggestions and submitted anyway, so precision is evaluable.
- TypeORM entity, mapper and migration, in the `incident` schema, with no foreign key to an Incident — the record exists where no Incident does.
- Out of scope: abandonment detection at the client (`T-C1-92`); any deflection reporting or rate calculation (`C17` / `C9`).

## Acceptance criteria

**Given** an abandonment signal naming an opened article **When** the use case executes **Then** exactly one deflection record is created with article, category and timestamp, and no Incident is created.

**Given** a requester who viewed suggestions and submitted anyway **When** the Incident is created **Then** no deflection record is written and the Incident records that suggestions were shown.

**Given** a deflection record **When** its contents are inspected **Then** it carries no free-text description and no personal data beyond what support needs (`NFR-SEC-07`).

**Given** the schema **When** it is inspected **Then** the deflection table has no non-nullable reference to an Incident, since the recorded case is precisely the one where none exists.
