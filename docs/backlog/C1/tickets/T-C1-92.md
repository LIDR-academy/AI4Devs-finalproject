# [C1] T-C1-92 · Abandonment detection at intake and the suggestions-shown marker

## Context

`US-C1-30` needs the client to tell the server which of two things happened: the requester **opened a suggested article and then abandoned the submission**, or **viewed suggestions and submitted anyway**. Only the browser can observe the first; the server records both (`T-C1-91`).

The Service Owner motivation is explicit: the value of the Knowledge Base should be **measured rather than assumed**.

## Scope

- Detection of the abandonment case on the intake form: a suggestion was opened and the form was left without submitting, reported once through `incident/data-access`.
- The suggestions-shown marker attached to the submission when the requester submits after seeing suggestions.
- At-most-once reporting: navigating away and back, or a retried request, does not produce two deflection records.
- No content and no personal data sent beyond the article reference and the category, matching `NFR-SEC-07`.
- Out of scope: the record and its persistence (`T-C1-91`); the suggestion panel itself (`T-C1-90`); any deflection reporting surface.

## Acceptance criteria

**Given** a requester who opens a suggestion and leaves without submitting **When** the abandonment is detected **Then** exactly one deflection signal is sent, naming the article and the category.

**Given** the same requester returning and leaving again **When** detection runs **Then** no second deflection record results for the same session and article.

**Given** a requester who viewed suggestions and submitted **When** the Incident is created **Then** the submission carries the suggestions-shown marker and no deflection signal is sent.

**Given** the deflection signal **When** its payload is inspected **Then** it contains the article reference, the category and nothing else that identifies the requester beyond what support needs.
