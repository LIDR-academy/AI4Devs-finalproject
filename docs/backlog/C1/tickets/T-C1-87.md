# [C1] T-C1-87 · Intake evaluation: reject or flag before the record is created, with the redirection recorded

## Context

`US-C1-28` requires the system to reject or flag an in-application sport decision — a reschedule, a roster change, a result dispute, a sanction — and offer the correct path **before the record is created**; requires the configured behavior to apply **deterministically**; requires the flag to be recorded on the Incident where submission is allowed, **so the false-positive rate is measurable**; and requires a followed redirection to be recorded, giving the Product Owner evidence of demand pressure against the scope rule (risk **R1**).

**Blocked by finding F25**, as `T-C1-86` is: reject-versus-flag is assumed configurable and that assumption needs confirmation. The evaluation mechanism is buildable under either decision.

## Scope

- Scope evaluation invoked from the intake use cases of `T-C1-07` and `T-C1-11`, **before** the aggregate is created.
- Reject outcome: a typed refusal carrying the matched demand category and the offered path, with no Incident created.
- Flag outcome: the Incident is created and carries the scope flag and the matched category, so precision is measurable later.
- `ScopeRedirectionRecorded` entity and event capturing the matched rule, the offered path and whether the requester followed it — written independently of the Incident, since a rejected submission creates none.
- Out of scope: the rules themselves (`T-C1-86`); the requester surface (`T-C1-88`); the Knowledge Article and Service Catalog targets (`C9`, `C8`); any reporting surface (`C17`).

## Acceptance criteria

**Given** a submission matching a rule configured to reject **When** it is submitted **Then** no Incident is created and the refusal names the offered path.

**Given** a submission matching a rule configured to flag **When** the requester proceeds **Then** the Incident is created carrying the scope flag and the matched category.

**Given** the same submission and the same configuration **When** it is evaluated repeatedly **Then** the outcome is identical every time — the behavior is deterministic, not heuristic-dependent.

**Given** a redirected submission **When** the requester follows the offered path **Then** the redirection is recorded with the matched rule and the path, independently of any Incident.

**Given** the evaluation **When** it runs **Then** it happens before the aggregate is constructed, asserted by the absence of any persisted Incident on the reject path.
