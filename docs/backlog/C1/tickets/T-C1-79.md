# [C1] T-C1-79 · FCR derived from assignment history and resolving tier

## Context

`US-C1-32` requires First Contact Resolution to be **derived from what actually happened on the ticket**, computed by the system and **not settable by an agent**, so the metric cannot be inflated by ticking a box. An Incident **reassigned at any point, or resolved by a tier other than L1, does not qualify**, and the reason it did not qualify must be derivable from the assignment history.

**Blocked by finding F28.** `FR-INC-18` does not define **“the first interaction”** — whether a public comment, a callback, a requester reply or an elapsed period ends it is unstated, and FCR is one of the stated MVP acceptance outputs. This ticket delivers the two **unambiguous** halves: *no reassignment* and *resolved by L1*. The interaction boundary stays behind a single named predicate that raises a typed not-yet-defined outcome until the Product Owner settles it. Until then, **FCR is not implementable to a testable definition** and must not be reported as complete.

## Scope

- `FirstContactResolution` derivation in `libs/incident/domain` reading the assignment history of `T-C1-72` and the resolving tier.
- The two decided conditions implemented and exhaustively unit-tested.
- The interaction-boundary condition isolated behind one named predicate, documented as pending **F28**, returning an explicit undefined outcome rather than a guessed true or false.
- A non-qualification reason emitted alongside the outcome, so reporting can say *why*.
- Out of scope: choosing the interaction boundary — a Product Owner decision; recording the outcome (`T-C1-80`); any reporting surface (`C17`).

## Acceptance criteria

**Given** an Incident reassigned at least once **When** FCR is derived **Then** it does not qualify, and the reason names the reassignment.

**Given** an Incident resolved by a tier other than L1 **When** FCR is derived **Then** it does not qualify, and the reason names the resolving tier.

**Given** an Incident with no reassignment resolved by L1 **When** FCR is derived **Then** the interaction-boundary predicate is consulted and, while **F28** is unresolved, the outcome is the explicit undefined result — never a default true.

**Given** the interaction boundary **When** the domain library is searched **Then** it is stated once, in one predicate, documented as pending **F28**; no reporting or application code answers the question independently.
