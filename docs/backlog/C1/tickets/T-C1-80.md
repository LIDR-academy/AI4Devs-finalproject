# [C1] T-C1-80 · FCR recorded at resolution as a derived, non-settable fact, including the reopen rule

## Context

`US-C1-32` requires FCR to be **recorded on the Incident as a derived fact when it reaches `Resolved`**, and requires that an Incident which qualified and is later reopened per `US-C1-18` follows **a single documented rule for reopened Incidents, exercised by an explicit test, so the metric cannot be quietly inflated by a reopen-and-reclose cycle**.

**Blocked by finding F28**, as `T-C1-79` is: the recording mechanism is buildable, but what it records is undefined until the interaction boundary is settled.

The reopen rule is the part most easily got wrong. A reopen-and-reclose cycle that re-evaluates FCR from scratch would let any ticket eventually qualify.

## Scope

- FCR evaluated and persisted at each entry into `Resolved`, through the derivation of `T-C1-79`.
- No inbound path accepts FCR as an input: it is absent from every command shape and every DTO, asserted by an enumeration like the one in `T-C1-44`.
- Reopen rule stated once and applied: an Incident that has been reopened cannot subsequently qualify, and the recorded outcome and its reason are readable across the whole lifecycle.
- Persistence, mapper and migration for the outcome and its non-qualification reason.
- Out of scope: the interaction boundary decision (**F28**); the FCR metric surface (`C17`).

## Acceptance criteria

**Given** an Incident reaching `Resolved` **When** the transition commits **Then** the FCR outcome and, where it did not qualify, its reason are persisted as derived facts.

**Given** any request that supplies an FCR value **When** the route table is enumerated **Then** no route accepts it, and the enumeration fails if one is added.

**Given** an Incident that qualified, was reopened and was resolved again **When** FCR is re-evaluated **Then** the documented reopen rule applies, the outcome is recorded, and an explicit test asserts the reopen-and-reclose cycle cannot produce a qualification it did not earn.

**Given** **F28** unresolved **When** the epic is reviewed **Then** this ticket and `T-C1-79` are reported blocked and `FR-INC-18` is not claimed satisfied.
