# [C1] T-C1-77 · Hierarchical escalation to the management contact

## Context

`US-C1-26` requires an agent hierarchical escalation to target **the management contact rather than a support tier**, to be recorded as a **distinct escalation kind from `US-C1-25`**, and to notify through `C16`.

Management contacts are a `C14` / `C10` concern in the `identity-access` context; they are referenced through a port bound at the composition root, never imported.

## Scope

- Hierarchical kind added to `EscalationKind` from `T-C1-75`.
- `EscalateHierarchicallyUseCase` in `libs/incident/application`, permission-gated, targeting the management contact resolved through a port.
- The same `IncidentEscalated` event shape with the hierarchical kind, so `C16` and `C18` need no second event type.
- Contract DTOs and the HTTP route.
- Out of scope: management contact definition (`C14` / `C10`); notification delivery (`C16`); the automatic trigger (`T-C1-78`).

## Acceptance criteria

**Given** an agent with the escalation permission **When** they escalate hierarchically **Then** the target is the management contact, not a support tier, and the event carries the hierarchical kind.

**Given** a functional and a hierarchical escalation on the same Incident **When** the history is read **Then** the two are distinguishable by kind.

**Given** no management contact resolvable **When** the escalation is attempted **Then** it is refused with a typed error naming the reason, rather than silently escalating to nobody.

**Given** an actor without the escalation permission **When** they attempt it **Then** it is denied with `403` and nothing changes.
