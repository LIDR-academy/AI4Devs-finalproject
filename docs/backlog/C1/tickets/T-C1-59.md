# [C1] T-C1-59 · Confirmation window opened at resolution through `ClockPort`

## Context

`US-C1-16` requires the confirmation window of `US-C1-18` to start when the Incident enters `Resolved`. `US-C1-18` then requires a confirm or reject **inside** the window to be honoured and one **after** it to be refused with a message stating that the Incident is closed and what to do instead — never a silent failure.

The window boundary must be computed from persisted UTC timestamps via `ClockPort` (ADR-009), which is what makes both this ticket and the auto-close of `T-C1-63` testable on `FixedClock` and correct across a restart.

## Scope

- Confirmation window opened on the aggregate at each entry into `Resolved`, anchored to the resolution timestamp.
- A pure predicate answering whether the window is open at a given instant, shared by the confirm, reject and auto-close paths so all three agree by construction.
- The window re-opens on re-resolution after a reopen, anchored to the new resolution.
- Out of scope: the confirm and reject use cases (`T-C1-60`); the configured duration and the auto-close rule (`T-C1-63`).

## Acceptance criteria

**Given** an Incident entering `Resolved` **When** the aggregate is read **Then** a confirmation window is open, anchored to the resolution timestamp taken from `ClockPort`.

**Given** an instant inside, exactly at, and outside the window boundary **When** the predicate is evaluated on `FixedClock` **Then** each of the three cases returns a defined, asserted result.

**Given** an Incident resolved, reopened and resolved again **When** the window is read **Then** it is anchored to the second resolution, not to the first.

**Given** the predicate **When** the confirm, reject and auto-close paths are inspected **Then** all three call it, and none contains its own boundary arithmetic.
