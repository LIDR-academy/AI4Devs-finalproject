# [C10] T-C10-45 · Organizer competition-scoped visibility rule

## Context

`US-C10-07` requires a Tournament Organizer / Admin to see records affecting the competitions they hold a grant over, **on top of** their own records, and to see nothing beyond their own records when they hold no grant at all.

Finding **F15** applies: this is verified as a predicate, by unit test, with no ticket aggregate present.

## Scope

- Extend `RecordVisibilityPolicy` from `T-C10-41` with the Organizer rule, evaluated against the actor grants from `T-C10-43`.
- The rule is additive: it never removes visibility the requester rule already granted.
- The yielded `ScopeRestriction` widens to own records plus the granted competition set, still expressed declaratively for the consuming repository.
- Jest unit tests: granted competition allowed, ungranted competition denied, no grants behaves exactly as the plain requester rule of `US-C10-06`, and act-upon follows view.
- Out of scope: the league rule (`T-C10-46`); persistence (`T-C10-44`).

## Acceptance criteria

**Given** an actor holding the Organizer role with an explicit grant over competition `X` **When** the predicate is evaluated for a record whose affected competition is `X` **Then** it grants visibility, even though the actor is not the requester of that record.

**Given** the same actor **When** the predicate is evaluated for a record whose affected competition is `Y`, over which they hold no grant **Then** it denies.

**Given** an Organizer with no competition grants at all **When** the predicate is evaluated **Then** it behaves exactly as the plain requester rule, granting nothing beyond their own records.

**Given** an Organizer with a grant **When** the restriction is produced for a list query **Then** it names both the own-records clause and the granted competition set, and the two are a union rather than a replacement.

**Given** the rule **When** its source is read **Then** the grant is consulted from the actor grants and never inferred from a role label or a name match.
