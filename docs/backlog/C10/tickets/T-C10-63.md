# [C10] T-C10-63 · Denial sink adapter and its destination

## Context

**Blocked by finding F17 — this ticket cannot be implemented as written.** `FR-IAM-07` says a denied authorization must be *recorded*, but never says where. A denied authorization is not a change to a record: it has no previous value, no new value and no natural record reference, so it does not fit the `AuditEntry` shape of `FR-AUD-02`. The alternative is a dedicated security log owned by `identity-access`. `US-C10-16` deliberately specifies the content of the denial record but not its store.

**This is an architecture decision.** It must be made by the Architect, with the Product Owner confirming the retention and access expectations, before this ticket is estimated as real work. The estimate below is the cost of the adapter under either option and will move once the option is chosen.

Do not implement this by guessing. `T-C10-62` is complete without it: the port exists and the record is produced; only the sink is open.

Phase: `unphased` (`FR-IAM-07`, finding **F9**).

## Scope — conditional on the F17 decision

- **If a `C18` audit entry is chosen:** an adapter in `apps/api` translating `AuthorizationDenial` into the `C18` append-only entry, accepting that previous value and new value are absent, and a `C18`-side agreement that such an entry is legal. Adds a `C10` to `C18` dependency beyond the publish-only relationship of finding **F5**.
- **If a dedicated security log is chosen:** a `identity_access.authorization_denial` table with its migration, an append-only repository with no update and no delete method, and a retention rule.
- Either way: the port from `T-C10-62` is the seam, so the choice does not reach the application layer.
- Out of scope: making the decision.

## Acceptance criteria

Not yet writable as behavior. Blocking questions to close first:

**Given** the two options above **When** the Architect decides **Then** the destination is recorded as an ADR naming the store, its immutability guarantee and its retention period.

**Given** that decision **When** it is made **Then** this ticket is rewritten with concrete acceptance criteria and re-estimated, and the `blocked_by` marker is removed.

**Given** the decision is deferred **When** the epic ships **Then** `US-C10-16` is delivered only as far as `T-C10-62` — the record is produced and handed to a port with no bound sink — and `FR-IAM-07` remains unsatisfied. That state must be reported, not silently accepted.
