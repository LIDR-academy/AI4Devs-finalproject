# [C10] T-C10-46 · League Administrator scope resolved through the league

## Context

`US-C10-08` requires a League Administrator to see the union of the competitions in the leagues they oversee — an escalation contact, **not** a read-all grant — and requires a competition newly added to such a league to be included without any change to the actor grants, because the scope resolves through the league at evaluation time.

The story also requires the League Admin scope to stay distinct from the Service Manager read-all permission of PRD §4.3: read-all is a permission on the role, the League Admin scope is a bounded set.

## Scope

- Extend `RecordVisibilityPolicy` with the league rule, resolving the league grant to its competition set through the port operation of `T-C10-43` on every evaluation.
- The resulting scope is the union of the resolved competition sets plus own records; it is never a wildcard.
- An explicit test contrasting the League Admin scope with the Service Manager read-all permission, so the two mechanisms cannot be conflated later.
- Jest unit tests with a stubbed grant repository, including the mid-test league membership change.
- Out of scope: the read-all permission itself, which is part of the role catalog (`T-C10-35`).

## Acceptance criteria

**Given** an actor holding the League Admin role with a grant over a league containing competitions `X` and `Y` **When** the predicate is evaluated for records affecting `X` or `Y` **Then** it grants visibility for both.

**Given** the same actor **When** the predicate is evaluated for a record affecting a competition outside their leagues **Then** it denies; the scope is a union of competition scopes, not a wildcard.

**Given** a competition added to a league the actor oversees **When** the predicate is next evaluated **Then** the new competition is included with no change to the actor grants.

**Given** the Service Manager read-all permission and the League Admin scope **When** they are compared in test **Then** read-all is asserted as a permission on the role and the League Admin scope as a bounded competition set, and neither is implemented in terms of the other.
