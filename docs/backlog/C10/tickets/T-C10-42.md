# [C10] T-C10-42 · Not-found-indistinguishable outcome on direct-identifier access

## Context

`US-C10-06` requires that when a requester asks for a record they may not see **by direct identifier**, the outcome is indistinguishable from the record not existing, so the response does not confirm its existence. That is a deliberate divergence from the `403` mapping of `T-C10-40` and must be applied consistently.

## Scope

- A single-record access helper in `libs/identity-access/application` that evaluates `RecordVisibilityPolicy` and, on denial, returns the same outcome as a genuine miss.
- The helper is the mechanism consuming contexts (`C1`, `C2`) will use, so the rule is written once here rather than restated per context.
- Documented rule: visibility denial on direct-identifier read maps to `404`; permission denial on a privileged operation maps to `403` (`T-C10-40`). The two are distinct and must not be merged.
- Jest unit tests covering: record absent, record present but denied, record present and allowed.
- Out of scope: the consuming contexts themselves (finding **F15**).

## Acceptance criteria

**Given** a record that does not exist **When** a requester reads it by identifier **Then** the outcome is a not-found result.

**Given** a record that exists but is denied by the visibility predicate **When** the same requester reads it by identifier **Then** the outcome is byte-identical to the not-found result above, including the error code.

**Given** the two outcomes **When** their response timing is compared **Then** neither path performs work the other does not, so timing does not disclose existence.

**Given** a privileged operation denied by permission **When** it is compared with a visibility denial **Then** the first is `403` and the second is `404`, and the difference is documented and tested.
